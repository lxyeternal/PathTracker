简体中文

漫迹没有自己的服务器，不收集你的任何数据。

你的轨迹、到访地点、旅程、照片和备注只保存在你的设备上，并通过你自己的 iCloud 账户在你的设备之间同步。开发者无法看到、访问或导出这些数据。

位置信息

在你允许后，App 会在前台和后台读取你的位置，用来画出你走过的路线、识别你停留的地点。手机静止时系统会暂停定位以节省电量。你可以随时在 App 的“设置”中关闭自动记录，或在 iOS“设置”中修改定位权限。

地点识别

为了显示地点名称、城市和国家，App 会把停留地点的坐标发送给 Apple 地图服务进行反向地理编码和兴趣点查询。这一过程由 Apple 处理，受 Apple 隐私政策约束，不包含你的身份信息。

健康与运动数据

如果你允许，App 会读取“健康”中的步数和步行距离，以及设备的运动状态（步行、骑行、乘车），只用于在 App 内展示统计。这些数据不会离开你的设备和 iCloud。

Apple Watch

在手表上开始记录时，App 会使用手表的位置画出路线，读取心率和距离用于显示，并把这次记录作为体能训练（含路线）保存到“健康”。记录通过你的 iPhone 保存到你自己的 iCloud，不经过任何服务器。

照片

如果你允许，App 会在时间线上显示你在每个地点拍的照片。照片只在你的设备上读取，不会被复制、上传或同步。

使用“从相册点亮足迹”时，App 在你的设备上读取照片和视频的拍摄时间和地点，据此生成到访记录（只有时间和坐标）。照片本身不会被复制或上传。为了显示城市和国家，这些坐标会交给 Apple 地图服务识别。

你主动添加到旅程中的照片会被压缩后保存在 App 中并同步到你的 iCloud。

个人资料与 Apple 登录

你可以填写名字、邮箱、一句话简介和头像。这些资料只保存在你的设备和你的 iCloud 中。登录是可选的；使用“通过 Apple 登录”时，App 只额外保存 Apple 提供的匿名用户标识，以及你选择分享的名字和邮箱。

通知

到达新地方、达成里程碑和每周回顾的通知都在你的设备上生成，不经过任何服务器。

删除数据

你可以在“设置 › 删除所有数据”中永久删除所有轨迹、地点、旅程、照片和个人资料，删除会同步到你的所有设备和 iCloud。卸载 App 会删除本机数据；iCloud 中的数据可以在 iOS“设置 › Apple 账户 › iCloud › 管理存储空间”中删除。

第三方

App 不包含广告、数据分析或第三方追踪 SDK，不会出售或共享你的任何数据。

联系我们

如果你对隐私有任何疑问，请发送邮件至 honywendev@gmail.com。

English

Waytrace has no server of its own and collects none of your data.

Your tracks, visited places, trips, photos and notes are stored only on your devices and synced between them through your own iCloud account. The developer cannot see, access or export them.

Location

With your permission, the app reads your location in the foreground and background to draw the routes you take and recognize the places you stop. When the phone is still, the system pauses location to save power. You can turn off automatic recording in the app's Settings at any time, or change location access in iOS Settings.

Naming places

To show place names, cities and countries, the app sends the coordinates of the places you stop to Apple's map service for reverse geocoding and points of interest. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy and includes no information about who you are.

Health and motion

With your permission, the app reads steps and walking distance from Health, and the device's motion activity (walking, cycling, in a vehicle), only to show statistics in the app. This data never leaves your devices and iCloud.

Apple Watch

When you record on the watch, the app uses the watch's location to draw the route, reads heart rate and distance to display them, and saves the recording to Health as a workout with its route. Recordings are saved to your own iCloud through your iPhone, never through a server.

Photos

With your permission, the app shows the photos you took at each place on your timeline. Photos are read on your device only and are never copied, uploaded or synced.

When you use "Light Up from Photos", the app reads the time and location of your photos and videos on your device and creates visits from them (times and coordinates only). The photos themselves are never copied or uploaded. To show cities and countries, those coordinates are named by Apple's map service.

Photos you add to a trip yourself are compressed, stored in the app and synced to your iCloud.

Profile and Sign in with Apple

You can enter a name, email, a short bio and a photo. These stay on your devices and in your iCloud. Signing in is optional; with Sign in with Apple, the app additionally keeps only the anonymous user identifier Apple provides and the name and email you choose to share.

Notifications

Notifications for new places, milestones and the weekly recap are created on your device and never go through a server.

Deleting your data

Settings › Delete All Data permanently removes all tracks, places, trips, photos and your profile, on all your devices and in iCloud. Deleting the app removes the data on that device; data in iCloud can be removed in iOS Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Storage.

Third parties

The app contains no ads, analytics or third-party tracking SDKs, and never sells or shares any of your data.

Contact

For any privacy question, email honywendev@gmail.com.
